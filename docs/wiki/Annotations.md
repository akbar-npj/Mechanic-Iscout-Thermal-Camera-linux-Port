# Notes and arrows (Mark)

A **Mark** is a note you draw on top of the picture — a text label or an arrow.
Unlike the measuring tools, a Mark is not a measurement: it does not read a
temperature. It is there to point at something and say what it is.

Marks live in the **Mark** group of the Troubleshoot tab, on the rail's **Mark**
icon, and on the keyboard.

## Placing a text label

1. Press **`T`** (capital T) to arm the **Text** tool, or click the **Text** row.
2. **Click** on the spot you want to label.
3. Type the text in the box that appears and confirm.

The label lands on the exact pixel you clicked, with a small leader line down to a
dot on that spot. If you click outside the picture, nothing is placed and the tool
goes away. If you cancel the text box, the tool stays armed so you can try again.

## Drawing an arrow

1. Press **`w`** to arm the **Arrow** tool, or click the **Arrow** row.
2. **Press, drag and release** to draw the arrow from its tail to its head.

The arrow follows your drag live, so you can see where it is going before you let
go. A press with no drag places nothing.

## The one-tool rule

Mark tools and measuring tools share a single "active tool". Arming a Mark puts
the measuring tool away, and picking a measuring tool clears the Mark. That is why
you cannot accidentally have a Spot probe and an Arrow armed at the same time.

## Undo, redo and reset

The Mark group also has **Undo**, **Redo** and **Reset**:

| Action | Keys | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Undo** | `Ctrl+Z` | Takes back the last Mark you placed |
| **Redo** | `Ctrl+Y` or `Ctrl+Shift+Z` | Puts back the last Mark you undid |
| **Reset** | `Ctrl+R` | Removes **all** Marks and clears the history |

Undo always removes the most recently placed Mark, whichever kind it is (text or
arrow). Placing a new Mark discards anything you had undone but not redone. Reset
is not "undo everything" — it wipes the marks *and* the history, so there is
nothing left to redo back to.

The three rows grey out when there is nothing to do: an Undo with an empty history
would be a button that does nothing.

## The rail's Mark icon

The **Mark** icon on the left rail is a shortcut to the tools:

- **Left-click** re-arms the tool you were last using (not always Spot).
- **Right-click** opens a small menu with the two kinds — **Text** and
  **Arrow** — and, below a separator, the **Undo / Redo / Reset** actions.

This is the one place both kinds of Mark are offered without using the keyboard.
